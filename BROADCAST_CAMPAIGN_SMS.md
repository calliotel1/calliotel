## 📞 SUPPORT RESPONSES

**If users ask: "How do I disable SMS?"**
```
Go to Profile > SMS Settings > Toggle off the notifications you don't want. 
You can also remove your phone number anytime.
```

**If users ask: "Why am I not receiving SMS?"**
```
Check:
1. Phone number added in Profile > SMS Settings?
2. SMS notifications enabled for that type?
3. You're Gold tier or higher? (Bronze/Silver don't have SMS)
4. You haven't exceeded your monthly quota? (Gold = 20/month)
```

**If users ask: "Does this cost money?"**
```
No SMS fees from us! Gold includes 20 SMS/month, Platinum = unlimited. 
Standard carrier rates may apply from your mobile provider.
```
