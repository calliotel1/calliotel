## 🚀 STEP-BY-STEP DEPLOYMENT PROCESS

### 🔥 OPTION 1: PWABUILDER (EASIEST - RECOMMENDED!)

This is the **easiest** way to create your Android APK!

#### Step 1: Go to PWABuilder
- Open your browser
- Visit: **https://www.pwabuilder.com/**

#### Step 2: Enter Your App URL
- In the box, type: `https://call-management-3.preview.emergentagent.com`
- Click **"Start"**
- Wait 10-20 seconds while it analyzes your app

#### Step 3: Package Your App
- Click **"Package For Stores"** button
- Select **"Android"**
- Click **"Generate"**
- Wait for the build to complete (1-2 minutes)

#### Step 4: Download Your APK
- Click **"Download"** when ready
- You'll get a ZIP file with your signed APK!
- Extract the ZIP file to find `app-release-signed.apk`

✅ **Your APK is ready!** Now let's upload it to Google Play Store.
